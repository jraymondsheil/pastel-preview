Part one: Brand

01

Brand identity

Naming, positioning, look and voice

A brand identity is everything that makes a community recognizable: its name, its colors and type, the way it sounds in writing. We create it once, then make sure it holds together on the sign, the website and every ad.

What is included

  • Naming and tagline options, checked for availability
  • Positioning: who the property is for and why they would choose it
  • Color palette, typography and photography direction
  • Voice and sample wording for the website and ads
  • A brand guide your team and vendors can follow
  • Templates for signage, brochures and leasing materials

Best for: new developments and lease-ups, communities changing hands or being renovated, and management companies whose own brand has fallen behind their portfolio.

02

Logo development

A mark, a wordmark and every file you need

The logo is the part of the brand people see first and most often. We draw it to be distinctive and simple, so it reads as clearly on a monument sign as it does in a browser tab.

What is included

  • Several concept directions to choose between
  • Rounds of refinement on the direction you pick
  • Primary logo, wordmark and a compact icon
  • Full color, one color and reverse versions
  • Files for print, web and signage vendors
  • Simple rules for spacing, sizing and use

Best for: properties that need a new mark without a full rebrand, or as the first step of a brand identity project.

Part two: Websites

03

Websites

Property websites and company portfolio websites

Most renters and buyers meet a property online before they ever visit. We design and build websites that answer their questions quickly and make it easy to get in touch, on a platform your own team can update in minutes.

What is included

  • Design and build in your brand, for phone and desktop
  • Floor plans, pricing and availability from your property management software
  • Photo galleries, amenities, neighborhood and map pages
  • Inquiry forms routed to the right leasing team
  • FAQs and a chat assistant that answer from your own content
  • An editor for your team: specials, photos and text, published on save
  • Hosting, security and upkeep handled by us
  • Portfolio sites that list every community and link to each one

Best for: any community or management company whose website is dated, hard to update, or not producing inquiries.

Part three: Marketing

04

Search engine optimization

SEO: being found without paying for the click

When someone searches "apartments near me" or the name of your neighborhood, SEO is what decides whether your property appears. It is steady work on your website and your Google listing that earns visits you do not pay for one by one.

What is included

  • Research into what people in your market search for
  • Page titles, wording and structure tuned to those searches
  • Google Business Profile set up, corrected and kept active
  • Consistent name, address and phone across listing sites
  • Site speed and technical health checks
  • A monthly report in plain language

Best for: stabilized communities that want a dependable flow of inquiries, and every new website we launch.

05

Search engine marketing

SEM: paid search ads, also called pay-per-click

SEM places your property at the top of the search results as an ad, from the day a campaign starts. You set the advertising budget. We build the campaigns and adjust them as your vacancies change.

What is included

  • Campaigns built around your floor plans and neighborhood
  • Ad wording written and tested by us
  • Budgets shifted toward the homes you most need to fill
  • Tracking of calls and form inquiries, not just clicks
  • A monthly report: what was spent and what it produced

Best for: lease-ups, seasonal slowdowns, and any property that needs inquiries sooner than SEO alone can deliver.

06

Social media marketing

SMM: organic posts and paid social

Social media is where people get a feel for a place before they tour it. We plan and produce posts in your brand, and run paid campaigns that put your community in front of people in your market.

What is included

  • A monthly content calendar, approved by you
  • Designed posts, photos and short video in your brand
  • Paid campaigns on the platforms your audience uses
  • Profiles set up and kept consistent with your website
  • A monthly report on reach and inquiries

Best for: communities with a story to show, such as new amenities, renovations, events or a strong neighborhood.

07

Geotargeting

Location-based advertising, including geofencing

Geotargeting shows your ads to people based on where they are or where they have been. We draw the areas that matter to your property and deliver ads to phones and screens inside them.

What is included

  • A map of target areas: around your property, major employers, campuses and commuter routes
  • Display and mobile ads designed in your brand
  • Follow-up ads for people who have visited your website
  • Reporting on views, clicks and visits to your website
  • Campaigns planned within fair housing advertising rules

Best for: new openings that need local awareness quickly, and properties competing for the same renters in a crowded area.
